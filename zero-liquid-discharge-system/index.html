Zero Liquid Discharge

MEGA Engineering designs and builds zero liquid discharge (ZLD) systems for distilleries and process industries in India and worldwide. We combine biomethanation, multiple effect evaporation, drying and condensate polishing into one engineered plant that recovers water for reuse and turns dissolved solids into a dry product.

Why Zero Liquid Discharge

Turn Effluent into Water, Energy and Product

High-strength effluent such as distillery spent wash cannot be discharged. A well-engineered ZLD plant meets the regulations and recovers value from every stream.

  1. 01

    Regulation

    Zero liquid discharge is now the norm for molasses distilleries, and discharge limits keep tightening for other industries.

  2. 02

    Water Recovery

    Evaporator condensate, once polished, replaces fresh water as process and cooling-tower make-up.

  3. 03

    Energy from Effluent

    Biomethanation converts the organic load into biogas that can fire the boiler or the dryer's hot air generator.

  4. 04

    Saleable By-Products

    Dissolved solids leave as potash-rich powder or cattle-feed concentrate instead of lagoon sludge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is a Zero Liquid Discharge System?

A zero liquid discharge (ZLD) system treats industrial effluent so that no liquid leaves the site: the water is recovered and reused, and the dissolved solids leave as a dry product, concentrate or ash.

How Does ZLD Work in a Distillery?

MEGA delivers two routes. In the biogas route, spent wash is digested to biogas, then degassed and concentrated in a multiple effect evaporator and dried to powder or concentrated to cattle-feed concentrate. In the incineration route, raw spent wash is concentrated to 55–60 Brix and burnt in a boiler. In both, the evaporator condensate is polished and recycled.

What Equipment Is Typically Included in a ZLD Plant?

Depending on the effluent: pretreatment, biomethanation, degassing, multiple effect evaporation with TVR or MVR, an agitated thin film dryer or spray dryer, and a condensate polishing unit.

How Is Condensate Reused?

Evaporator condensate is treated by stripping, biological treatment, filtration and membranes as needed to reach the target quality, then returned as process or cooling-tower make-up water.

Which Industries Need ZLD?

Zero liquid discharge is now the norm for molasses distilleries, and is also used by sugar, chemical, pharmaceutical and other industries with high-strength or high-TDS effluent.

Can ZLD Be Added to an Existing Plant?

Yes. We start with effluent characterisation and a water and energy balance of the existing plant, then design the evaporation, drying and condensate treatment around it.
